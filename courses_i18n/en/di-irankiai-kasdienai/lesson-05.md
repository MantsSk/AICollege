# Decisions, Plans, and Daily Productivity

AI helps when you need to structure a messy situation: what to choose, where to start, what to compare, and what risks matter.

## Decision Table

```text
Help me compare these options: [A] and [B].

Criteria:
- cost;
- time;
- risk;
- long-term value;
- constraints that matter in my situation.

Provide a table, a recommendation, and when that recommendation would be wrong.
```

## Weekly Plan

```text
Help me plan my week.
My goals: [goals].
Constraints: [time, energy, existing tasks].

Provide:
1. priorities;
2. a realistic weekly plan;
3. what to postpone;
4. one small step for today.
```

## Idea Generation

A good idea prompt asks not only for a list, but also selection:

```text
Generate 20 ideas for [goal].
Then choose the best 5 by:
- simplicity;
- cost;
- likelihood I will actually do it;
- possible benefit.
```

## Mini Project

Choose one real decision: a purchase, trip, study plan, work process, or personal goal. Ask AI to create a table, then edit the criteria yourself and make the final decision.

> **Mentor tip:** Ask "Help me make a decision, but first clarify my criteria."
