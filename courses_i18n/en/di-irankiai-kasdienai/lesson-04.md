# AI as a Learning Coach

AI can be a strong learning assistant if you do not use it only to copy answers. The best mode: AI asks questions, explains, gives hints, and checks your understanding.

**After this lesson you will be able to:**

- switch on "coach mode" where AI asks instead of answering;
- build a realistic study plan with self-checks;
- use AI to test your understanding, not just to get answers.

## Explanation Prompt

```text
Explain [topic] to a beginner.
Use:
- a simple analogy;
- one practical example;
- 3 common mistakes;
- 5 self-check questions.
```

## Coach Mode

```text
Be my learning coach.
Do not answer everything at once.
Ask one question at a time.
If I am wrong, give a hint, not the final answer.
At the end, assess what I understand and what I should repeat.
```

## Study Plan

```text
Create a 14-day study plan for: [topic].
I can spend [x] minutes per day.
For each day, provide:
- goal;
- short explanation;
- practical task;
- self-check question.
```

## Check yourself

**1. How does coach mode differ from ordinary question-and-answer?**

<details class="selfcheck" markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

A coach does not answer everything at once: it asks one question at a time and gives hints instead of answers when you are wrong. The learning happens in your head, not on the screen.

</details>

**2. Why does the study plan need your available minutes per day?**

<details class="selfcheck" markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Without that constraint, AI builds an "ideal" but unrealistic plan. A realistic plan you stick to beats an ambitious one you abandon after three days.

</details>

**3. What is the biggest danger of learning with AI?**

<details class="selfcheck" markdown="1">
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Copying answers instead of thinking — it feels like learning, but no understanding forms. A good mode always includes your attempt and its review.

</details>

## Mini Project

Create a study plan for one real topic: English, Excel, programming, financial literacy, or anything else. Then ask AI to create the first day's task and check your answer.

> **Task check:** do the first day's task and send the mentor your answer: "Check my answer as a coach: what is good, where am I wrong, what should I repeat tomorrow?"

> **Mentor tip:** Ask "Teach me this topic as a coach, but do not let me simply copy the answer."
