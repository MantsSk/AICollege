# Privacy, Ethics, and Safe Use

AI tools are becoming everyday tools, so it is easy to forget that we often send them text, documents, photos, names, work information, or sensitive plans. Responsible use starts with one question: what am I giving the system?

**After this lesson you will be able to:**

- tell which data should not go into AI tools;
- anonymize sensitive text before sending it to a model;
- check your organization's rules before using AI at work.

## What you should avoid uploading

Without a clear reason and the right settings, do not upload:

- passwords, API keys, login details;
- personal IDs, bank details, medical documents;
- confidential work documents;
- other people's private data without permission;
- documents you could not show to an outside person.

If you need help with sensitive text, you can often **anonymize** it:

```text
Use placeholders instead of real names: Client A, Employee B, Company C.
Remove addresses, IDs, phone numbers, and exact dates.
```

## Data-use questions

Different AI tools have different rules: some may use conversations to improve models, some let you turn that off, and some business products have stricter protections.

Before using AI for work, check:

- whether your organization allows the tool;
- whether internal documents may be uploaded;
- whether conversations are used for training;
- where data is stored;
- whether a contract or business plan is required.

## Bias

Models learn from human-created data, so they can repeat stereotypes, uneven representation, or cultural assumptions.

A useful prompt:

```text
Review this answer for possible bias.
Are any groups unfairly ignored?
Could any wording be misunderstood?
Suggest a more neutral version.
```

## Authorship and honesty

AI can help you write, but it is worth separating:

- your ideas;
- AI's suggested wording;
- facts from sources;
- quotes or data that must be cited.

In learning, it is risky to use AI in a way that replaces thinking. Use it as a coach instead: let it ask questions, explain, give hints, and review your attempt.

## Safe-use checklist

Before sending text to AI, ask:

- does this contain sensitive data?
- do I have the right to upload this information?
- could the answer affect health, money, law, or reputation?
- how will I verify the result?
- does a human need to review it?
