# Your Everyday AI Workflow

AI becomes most useful when you have a simple workflow. Not one perfect prompt, but a habit: define the goal, give context, get a draft, verify, revise, and adapt.

**After this lesson you will be able to:**

- apply the six-step process to any AI task;
- use the universal prompt as a starting point;
- pull the whole course together in one final exercise.

## The six-step process

1. **Define the result.** What exactly do you want to have at the end?
2. **Give context.** Who is it for, what matters, what should be avoided?
3. **Ask for structure.** Let AI suggest a plan first.
4. **Create a draft.** Do not treat the first answer as final.
5. **Check and revise.** Look for errors, assumptions, and unclear parts.
6. **Make it yours.** Add your voice, judgment, and responsibility.

## A universal prompt

```text
I want to achieve: [goal].
Context: [audience, what I already have, important details].
Constraints: [deadline, tone, length, what to avoid].

First suggest a short plan.
Then prepare a first draft.
At the end, list what I should verify before using it.
```

## Example: learning

```text
I want to understand basic investing terms in 2 weeks.
I am a beginner and do not want complex math.
Create a study plan with 30 minutes per day.
For each day, give a topic, short explanation, and one practical task.
Mark what is not financial advice and what I should verify in reliable sources.
```

## Example: writing

```text
I need to write a polite email to a client about a delayed project.
Context: the delay is 3 days, caused by extra quality review.
Tone: responsible, calm, no blame.
Goal: inform, apologize, and suggest a new date.
Give 2 versions: short and more detailed.
```

## Example: decision-making

```text
Help me decide whether to buy a new laptop now or wait.
I care about: price, battery life, reliability, learning programming, 3 years of use.
Create a criteria table.
Give a recommendation and situations where it would be wrong.
```

## Final Exercise

Choose one real task and complete the full process:

1. write the goal;
2. create the prompt;
3. get the AI answer;
4. ask for critique;
5. revise the result;
6. write down what you verified yourself.

> **Task check:** describe to the mentor how you completed all six steps and ask: "Evaluate my process: which step was my weakest and how do I strengthen it?"

> **Mentor tip:** Ask "Help me create a personal AI use plan based on my work, learning, and hobbies."

Congratulations. You now have not just theory, but a practical system for using AI.
