# Images, Audio, and Multimodal AI

AI is no longer limited to text. Many modern systems can understand or create images, audio, video, spreadsheets, and documents. This is called **multimodal AI**: a system works with more than one type of information.

**After this lesson you will be able to:**

- use AI to understand images, documents, and audio recordings;
- write a clearer image-generation prompt;
- know where image analysis fails most often.

## What multimodal tools can do

- Describe a photo or chart.
- Read text inside an image.
- Help understand a spreadsheet or PDF.
- Create an illustration from a description.
- Transcribe audio into text.
- Translate or shorten a video transcript.
- Help create slides, posters, or social media content.

## Image analysis

When you upload an image, AI can notice objects, text, layout, and context. But it can still be wrong, especially when:

- the image is blurry;
- tiny details matter;
- medical, legal, or technical interpretation is required;
- you ask it to identify a person or sensitive information.

A good prompt:

```text
Describe what you see in this image.
Separate what you can clearly see from guesses.
If text is unreadable, say so.
```

## Image generation

When creating images, the most important details are:

- subject;
- style;
- composition;
- lighting;
- colors;
- format;
- what to avoid.

Example:

```text
Create a warm, realistic illustration of a person learning with an AI assistant at a kitchen table.
Style: modern editorial illustration, natural colors, clear composition.
Avoid futuristic robots and chaotic details.
```

## Audio and transcription

AI can transcribe a meeting, lecture, or voice note. After transcription, you can ask for:

- a summary;
- action items;
- decisions and open questions;
- tone analysis;
- translation.

Important: if the recording contains other people's voices, think about consent and privacy rules.

## Mini Exercise

Choose one image or document and ask AI:

```text
Describe the content.
Extract the most important details.
Mark where there may be uncertainty.
Suggest 3 follow-up questions I should ask.
```

> **Task check:** paste the AI's description to the mentor and ask: "Which parts of this description are observation and which are guesses I should verify?"
